Our Story

Rooted in the heart of Newmarket on Fergus, Co. Clare, our bakery was born from a simple belief: that good bread brings people together.

We honor traditional baking methods, allowing our sourdough to ferment slowly and sourcing our dairy and flour from local Irish farms. Every pastry is shaped by hand, and every loaf is baked with patience and passion.

Whether you're grabbing a quick coffee before heading to Shannon, or lingering over a scone on a quiet Sunday morning, we're here to offer a little slice of comfort to our community.
